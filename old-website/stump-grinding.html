Stump Grinding

 

Tree stumps can be unsightly, obtrusive and hazardous. Stump grinding is the most efficient and cost-effective solution to removing unwanted stumps from your property, to prevent re-growth and the spread of tree disease.

We have a fleet of stump grinding machines allowing us to access even the most difficult to reach stumps.

Our operators use safety barricades to protect surrounding houses, windows and gardens. We undertake Dial before you dig investigation before grinding your stump to safely identify and avoid underground infrastructure.

Stumps can become:

  • Trip hazards;
  • An obstacle for replanting;
  • Nesting sites for termites;
  • Unsightly in grassed areas; and
  • Reshoot into full sized trees with significantly reduced structural integrity.
